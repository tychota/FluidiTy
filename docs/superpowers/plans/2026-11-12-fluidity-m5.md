# FluidiTy M5 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Implement and qualify sand, snow, dry scenes and distinct-body contact, and qualify the coupled scene when production liquid integration is available.

**Architecture:** Share the local constitutive math between host and device. Test the principal-frame laws directly before connecting them to full 3D deformation and bounded grid stepping. Reuse MAC transfer primitives where their support and work contracts fit.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, CUDA, CMake, GoogleTest, Windows CPU and SM120 hardware.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-fluidity-design.md §§5.2, 8.1–8.2, 15.2 and the Blender M5 workflow.

## Global Constraints

- Base at 549fb3f844841b79d14b7beb9ecfb6db9727c976.
- Use Kirchhoff stress and reference volume. Require positive determinant and admissible tangents.
- Keep constitutive state trial-owned until acceptance. Stable unsigned 64-bit body IDs reserve zero. Cut-side identity is separate.
- Use world SI for contact. Count reactions once and retain incoming normal velocity for Coulomb friction.
- Require target CUDA execution for CUDA evidence. Keep branch local.

## Design choices

A constitutive-first approach provides closed-form oracles independent of transfer, contact, and scene setup. A scene-first implementation would exercise integration earlier but would make it harder to distinguish a wrong yield return from a grid or timestep defect. Keep the principal-frame update independent and connect it to full deformation only after its oracles pass.

## Q-SAND measurement protocol

Use the exact 30 degree material friction angle. Sample the final particle cloud
at 0.75 s, and also sample the same estimator at 0.70 s to measure settling
drift. Use atan2(maximum particle height above the floor, maximum horizontal
particle radius from the fixed release center). This is a radial envelope
estimate of the approximately centered pile. Record the final angle at
h = 0.075 and 0.0625 m, and use their absolute difference as a grid-sampling
sensitivity measure. Also record the absolute 0.70 to 0.75 s drift for each
resolution. Require 30 to 35 degrees at both resolutions and at most
2 degrees of drift in each. Keep physical particle spacing and geometry fixed
and run both transfer backends.
