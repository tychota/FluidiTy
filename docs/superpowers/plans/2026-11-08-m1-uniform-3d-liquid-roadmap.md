# M1 Uniform 3D Liquid Solver roadmap

The [canonical design](../specs/2026-11-07-fluidity-design.md) governs M1. The branch starts at fetched `origin/main`, `044479f9fe22a4177d0ac8eb8854e496fe7149c6` (PR #9). M1 delivers a CPU and CUDA uniform sparse single-liquid solver, committed cache and replay, and a measured 3D acceptance run. Each slice has its own plan, RED and GREEN records, independent task reviews and a fresh whole-branch review. Report the exact tested commit and separate formal, analytic, native/device and scene evidence.

The M0 reference and Lean pilots, [Windows/CUDA runs](../../native/toolchains.md), [coarse–fine qualification](../../adaptivity/README.md), and [Blender reader qualification](../../blender/README.md) establish results for their stated fixtures and configurations. The 2D operators and dense pressure solve are useful comparisons. For 3D, sparse blocks with unique low-face ownership implement the selected grid directly. A dense 3D array is useful as an independent small oracle, while explicit duplicated face halos would require a reconciliation rule and introduce duplicate physical flux degrees of freedom.

## M1 exit gates from the design

- **Free fall, §2.3 Q-FALL and §5.1.** Unconstrained acceleration within 0.1% of g for every supported energy setting with consistent old/new transfer history. Use an algebraic check over e ∈ [0,1] and scene samples including endpoints and default 0.6. Report the measured error per sample. Keep the stored and advecting paths distinct.
- **Hydrostatic and volume, Q-LIQ.** A 1 m column has speed strictly below 1e-3 m/s after 2 s. Volume drift is at most 1% over 10 s, with boundary/source flux and physical compression accounted separately. Fix the volume estimator and report lost volume/count with a declared absolute floor for small components.
- **Piston, Q-PISTON and §6.1.** Displaced volume agrees within 1% of integrated moving-wall flux. Use the same apertures and wall motion in assembly and correction. Exercise an open/free-surface piston for physical displacement and a sealed incompatible case for rejection. The positive-compliance pressure oracle is separate.
- **Static sphere and dynamic drop, §6.3.** Measure the static 2σ/R jump and parasitic-current convergence. The l=2 frequency is within ±5% of the stated two-density formula at declared amplitude, resolution and damping. Use ρ_out=0 for the baseline ambient Dirichlet model with no exterior inertia. Freeze static jump tolerance, refinement criteria, measurement windows and fitting method in the scene plan because the design supplies no numbers for them. Record capillary dt ≤ sqrt((ρ_a+ρ_b)h³/(4πσ)). Include the liquid energy-control dam-break look-book of §5.1 and declare its measurements before running.
- **Projection, §§6.1, 11.2.** Check true global residual and volume-scaled local defect, correction consistency, nullspace/compatibility, regularized free-surface distance (minimum θ=0.05), and at most two pressure dt retries. An incompatible physical constraint stops without dt retry.
- **Schema, reliability and replay, §§11, 14, 15.1.** Verify coherent acceptance, materialized resolved defaults, content fingerprints, manifest-last publish, portable inspection and same-artifact interrupted/restarted output. Exercise cancellation, kill, NaN, capacity, device failure and slow copy; count 50 crash/cancel/device-error injection trials with every published frame readable and 1,000 cumulative default-scene frames with zero unresolved retries, accepted nonfinite states or warnings. Distinguish local acceptance evidence from the supported-matrix release gate.
- **Throughput, §§2.1, 12, 15.2.** Measure target-device end-to-end elapsed time, cold initialization and drain, frames/substeps and particle updates per second, pressure iterations and phase times, and peak live/reserved allocation and output bytes for named workloads. Enforce the aggregate GPU ceiling min(0.70 × total, 0.80 × free at job start). Record CPU and CUDA separately. The design specifies measured M1 throughput and gives 32 s/frame as a candidate Simulate allocation for the reference job, with no separate numeric M1 speed threshold. The 10,800 s full-wave budget, 21,600 s temporary fallback, and ≤1% unexplained frame-10-to-240 live-memory growth on the fixed reference job are separately tracked later full-job gates. Keep microfixture timings separate from scene throughput.
