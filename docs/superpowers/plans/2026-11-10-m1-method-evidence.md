## Constraints and proof boundary

- Base tree 674a2733155b75a1b37d934774dca285cee3a337 from main 7ff295e6cee1f66a7dce0b7fe515b5095b5ee7f2. No production ports, new dependencies, scene runs or GPU jobs in this slice.
- Coordinates and face anchors are signed. For face component a, the a axis uses an integer lattice and the other two axes use a half shift. A cell with index i occupies [i h, (i+1) h) on each axis. At boundaries RT0 uses the positive-side cell for transverse selection and within-cell derivatives.
- Each face sample is a velocity in m/s. The true interpolation gradient is in s⁻¹ and is independent of any affine momentum fit. Pressure measures and aperture/wall fluxes are outside this complete-support experiment.
- Exact reindexing pairs B3'(s) = B2(s+1/2) − B2(s−1/2) with cell-centered B2 and the signed outward finite difference divided by h. Complete product support makes the tensor trace commute. RT0 has the same-cell discrete divergence in the cell interior and continuous normal velocity across an ordinary face. Its tangential jumps constrain its approximation near faces. All-quadratic has no such general identity.
- Lean's F1 already establishes the complete quadratic moments and affine reconstruction, F2 establishes unmasked deposition moments, F5/F6 cover pressure topology and the matched flux correction, F8 covers integer budgets, F9 covers transaction rollback and F10 covers signed tile inclusion. For the mixed operator, the next formal target is a finite reindexing lemma with explicitly zero exterior coefficients or an index domain containing both shifted supports. The 1D derivative identity and physical h factor are separate premises until formalized. Check the pinned tools before adding a theorem. Record unavailable tools or unproved premises. This slice measures the float64 identity and carries no new Lean claim.
- A cut aperture, wall flux, absent face, coarse/fine interface or mismatched time level invalidates direct substitution of the complete uniform result. Refuse a missing sample in the reference. Do not add ghost continuation to this slice.
