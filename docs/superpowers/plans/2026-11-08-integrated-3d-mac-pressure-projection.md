# Integrated 3D MAC Pressure Projection Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Qualify a bounded integrated 3D pressure projection on owned MAC faces, with independently checked CPU and CUDA solutions and exact F5/F6 contracts.

**Architecture:** Prepare one immutable table of oriented pressure links, then use it for the integrated system, deterministic component gauges, velocity correction, and the physical defect. Solve the gauge-reduced system by matrix-free multigrid-preconditioned conjugate gradients. An independent dense oracle covers small fixtures and never participates in either runtime solver.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, CUDA, Abseil, GoogleTest, uv/Python/NumPy, Conan 2, CMake/Ninja, Lean/mathlib and pinned independent checker.

**Spec:** [Canonical design](../specs/2026-11-07-fluidity-design.md) §§6.1, 11.2, 13 and 15, [F5 and F6](../../references/formal-contracts.md), and the [M1 roadmap](2026-11-08-m1-uniform-3d-liquid-roadmap.md). Base is PR #10 merge `e6855ce5f91b44dd85aa6d449b6ded762b1f7dce`.

## Global constraints and selected rulings

- A face points from its low-index tail to its high-index head. The lattice index names the head coordinate. The tail contributes outward incidence +1, the head −1. Face slots follow `SparseMacGrid3D::FaceSlot`. Require every adjacent face of each pressure cell to have an owner slot, including sealed faces. Use widened coordinates when deriving endpoints and high-face indices.
- Inputs are nonempty and bounded to 4096 pressure cells and 24576 distinct faces. Pressure cells are unique and ordered lexicographically (z,y,x); face inputs are unique and ordered by owned face slot. Callers supply exactly the union of their six incident faces. A missing exterior cell is a boundary, while a missing allocation is a setup error. Output vectors follow the input orders. Each entry point borrows only const inputs and returns a staged result on success.
- Use physical open area `a` in m², full area `h²`, and signed wall velocity. Build `pressure_to_velocity_scale = dt * inverse_density / distance` and `conductance = a * pressure_to_velocity_scale` once. Use `C = V / (K dt)`; positive infinity for K selects C=0. Require finite positive V and dt, V at most h³, finite inputs except the infinite K sentinel, valid physical area, and positive representable coefficients on active links. Fail closed on overflow, underflow to zero in active coefficients, or nonfinite arithmetic. Do not compute derivatives or infer geometry in this slice.
- An internal link has two pressure cells and an oriented jump. A Dirichlet link has exactly one pressure cell, positive open area and an exterior pressure, with no jump field. A sealed link has zero open area and one or two pressure cells. An internal zero-open link contributes no conductance and separates components. A closed face preserves the supplied predicted fluid velocity; wall flux uses full blocked area regardless of that velocity.
- RHS and correction share frozen links. Predicted outward flux includes `a u* + (h²−a) u_wall`. Internal jump lifting adds `−R k J` to b. Dirichlet lifting adds `k p_ext` to the incident cell. Correction adds `(dt β/d)(p_tail−p_head+J)` to u*, inserting p_ext at the missing endpoint. Report original residual b−Ap for every cell, including pins, and compute physical defect independently as `(R F_corrected + W − V s + C(p−p_old))/V`.
- Use positive-conductance internal edges to find components. A positive Dirichlet or compliance coefficient anchors the component. Check compatibility of an unanchored component with deterministic compensated FP64 sums of physical source and prescribed boundary wall flux before launching. Allow only accumulation roundoff bounded by `32 * eps64 * sum(abs(physical terms))`, with zero absolute floor. Reject incompatible physical input as `kFailedPrecondition`. Eliminate the lowest cell ordinal of each compatible unanchored component with pressure zero. Retain conductance to each eliminated pin in neighboring diagonals.
- Use FP32 fine-level operator products and iterative vectors, FP64 dot products, FP64 coarse levels, FP64 true residual and velocity correction. Warm-start from previous pressures, subtracting the pin's prior pressure inside each unanchored component. No explicit dense fine matrix in the production path. Use a mirrored edge graph and deterministic, symmetric multigrid V-cycle. Precompute coarsest factor in FP64. CUDA executes iteration, multigrid application, true residual and correction on device; host topology preparation and orchestration are allowed.
- Freeze gates at `||b−Ap||₂ ≤ 2e−6 m³/s + 2e−6 ||b||₂` and `max_i |r_i|/V_i ≤ 2e−5 s⁻¹`. The independently accumulated physical defect must satisfy the same local bound, and each signed residual/defect identity must agree within an FP64 roundoff envelope. Require positive finite iteration curvature, finite outputs, at most 256 iterations, and `kAborted` for an exhausted solver or failing stopping gate. Reject invalid input with `kInvalidArgument`, capacity with `kResourceExhausted` and unrepresentable arithmetic with `kOutOfRange`. No dt retry, physics-derived boundary geometry, persistent arenas, replay, or throughput claim in this slice.
- Follow AGENTS.md. Use locked uv, Conan 2, Ninja presets, clang-format 19.1.5, explicit CUDA 120-real and 120-virtual and the actual GPU. Require independent task reviews and raw RED/GREEN evidence. Commit tasks and review fixes separately. Retain raw logs in ignored output.

## Review focus

- Reject a missing high-face owner, duplicate face, incorrect boundary variant and signed endpoint overflow before solving (Task 2).
- Zero area can disconnect two cells while a moving blocked wall still supplies flux. An isolated positive-compliance component retains its mean pressure (Tasks 1 and 2).
- A small integrated residual can conceal a large local defect in a tiny cell. A mismatched correction scale must fail the physical identity (Tasks 1–3).
- A shallow solve may pass a recursive residual while the original gauge row still fails. Check true residuals for all original rows and transactional nonconvergence (Tasks 3 and 4).
- A host-only solver can accidentally satisfy a parity test. Require a successful device receipt, real multilevel device iteration, full case inventory, and named-pipe memcheck (Task 4).

### Task 2: Owned pressure links, integrated system and defect evaluation

**Produces:** In namespace `fluidity`, define `MacPressureCell3D {CellIndex3D cell_index; double fluid_volume; double target_divergence; double bulk_modulus; double previous_pressure;}`, `InternalMacPressureLink3D {double pressure_jump;}`, `DirichletMacPressureLink3D {double exterior_pressure;}`, and empty `SealedMacPressureLink3D`. Define `MacPressureFace3D {FaceIndex3D face_index; double open_area; double inverse_density; double pressure_gradient_distance; double predicted_normal_velocity; double wall_normal_velocity; std::variant<InternalMacPressureLink3D, DirichletMacPressureLink3D, SealedMacPressureLink3D> pressure_link;}`. Internal interfaces in `fluidity::pressure_internal`:

- `PrepareMacPressureSystem3D(const MacPressureInputs3D&) -> absl::StatusOr<PreparedMacPressureSystem3D>`. Prepared system owns original FP64 cell coefficients, oriented frozen links, original RHS, component ordinals, gauges and pressure ordering. Links include owned face slot, optional endpoint ordinals, both areas, pressure-to-velocity scale, conductance, prescribed wall velocity, predicted velocity, and jump or exterior pressure. No mutable views.
- `EvaluateMacPressure3D(const PreparedMacPressureSystem3D&, std::span<const double> candidate_cell_pressures) -> absl::StatusOr<MacPressureProjection3D>`. Result owns `cell_pressures`, `corrected_face_velocities`, `integrated_rhs`, `true_residuals`, `cell_constraint_defects`, `gauge_cell_ordinals` and `MacPressureDiagnostics3D diagnostics`. Diagnostics fields are `iteration_count`, `multigrid_level_count`, `true_residual_l2`, `max_volume_scaled_residual` and `max_cell_constraint_defect`. The first two fields are zero for candidate-only evaluation. Evaluation reports metrics without applying stopping tolerances.
