**Goal:** Qualify the bounded uniform-liquid boundary operators that supply physical pressure geometry, complete old/new transfer support, and continuous wall contact on CPU and CUDA.

**Architecture:** Freeze a single physical liquid signed-distance field and an obstacle pose for pressure geometry. ## Global constraints and selected rulings

- Use SI. Physical liquid signed distance is negative inside. Outward normal points toward increasing distance and a sphere has positive curvature 2/R. Use uniform MAC owned faces and validated geometry from slices 1 and 2.
- This qualification uses finite, common-version liquid signed-distance samples at canonical cell centers and corners. Use linear interpolation on a fixed triangulation of each face and the corresponding six tetrahedra from the cube's low to high body diagonal. Shared face diagonals agree across cells. Reject missing samples/owners and unrepresentable arithmetic. Rendering fields do not supply geometry.
- The initial certified obstacle set contains one translating unit-normal halfspace or finite-thickness parallel slab, or no obstacle. A slab is clipped by its two planes, including when both cuts lie inside a cell or face and no corner is inside. This bounded representation supports exact swept queries without a sampling certificate. Refuse a pressure cell with positive liquid volume on both disconnected sides of a slab; one scalar unknown cannot represent both. Arbitrary sampled moving obstacles, rotation, and overlapping obstacle unions need a separate geometry/collision certificate.
- Freeze geometry at the substep start. Open area is full face area minus solid-blocked area. Fluid volume is the intersection of liquid and nonsolid space. Select pressure cells by strictly negative center distance and positive liquid volume. Require an allocated owner for every incident face. Use full open area for active fluid faces; pressure boundary type is determined by liquid neighbors and solid closure. Pressure and correction consume the same frozen typed links. Solid velocities have the same positive-axis orientation as the MAC face. Keep prior pressure source, modulus and history separate from geometric volume.
- For one-sided air faces use `theta = phi_liquid/(phi_liquid-phi_air)`, clamp to [0.05,1], record every lower regularization, and pass `theta*h` as pressure gradient distance. Use inverse liquid density at the link. Set exterior pressure to ambient gauge pressure plus `sigma*curvature` and do not add a surface-force impulse or a second jump. Use centered gradient and Hessian of the physical signed-distance field at the adjacent liquid center, with `curvature = (|g|² tr(H) - gᵀHg)/|g|³`. Require dimensionless `|g| >= 1e-8` when positive sigma needs curvature. Reject missing/degenerate derivatives. Use the vacuum-inertia bound `sqrt(rho*h³/(4*pi*sigma))` for positive sigma. Zero sigma uses no curvature and supplies no capillary time limit.
- Compatible deposition excludes allocated samples that are on the wrong side or structurally ineligible before normalization. Missing allocation for a resolved stencil remains a failure. Existing face mass never enters deposition eligibility. For each component use `N'=N/S`, `grad N'=(grad N - N' sum_compatible grad N)/S`, center offsets by the weighted mean, and guard the actual mass/pure momentum contributions before nearest-even integer accumulation. Liquid particle stress remains zero. Range, capacity, signed quanta and transactional rules match the original complete-support API.
- Normalize positive face mass only when greater than an explicit finite nonnegative minimum. Mark low-mass validity independently of side labels. For each missing or incompatible G2P sample, fit a local affine field to finite donors of the same side and normal component within two cell widths. Normalize offsets by h, choose donors in owned slot order, solve the 4-column least-squares problem with deterministic rank-revealing QR, and require each accepted normalized pivot at least `1e-10` of the largest. Fit and fallback are evaluated separately at old/new times. At a solid wall, constrain the fitted vector normal component relative to that snapshot's wall velocity, then select the staggered component.
- On insufficient rank use the nearest same-side valid donor for a constant continuation, breaking exact distance ties by owned slot, or a finite compatible prescribed wall value. If neither exists, reject. Count fallback by particle and stage, set affine disabled, keep diagnostic true gradient from the full filled stencil, and suppress old retention delta for PIC fallback and low-mass fallback. Distinguish `new_pic-old_pic` from `particle_velocity-old_pic`. The slice qualifies ghost construction and boundary interaction, while complete energy-control/substep scheduling follows in slice 4.
- Continuous wall contact uses signed distance to the same translating halfspace/slab and a closed-form earliest intersection in relative motion. Sweep the advecting path before writing position; fixed sampling cannot certify a thin wall. Apply the one-way velocity response to stored and advecting velocities independently. Save each incoming relative normal component before clamping it and cap tangent reduction by Coulomb friction. Initial penetration beyond explicit tolerance, unsupported obstacle or ambiguous incompatible contact rejects. Preserve staged outputs on failure.
- Keep the slice bounded to 4096 pressure cells and 24576 owned face inputs from slice 2, and 1024 particles for this boundary parity path. No new dependencies. Follow AGENTS.md for C++20, domain names, API contracts and invariant comments. Preserve all existing APIs and receipts. Use locked uv, Conan 2, Ninja, the actual qualified GPU and named-pipe Compute Sanitizer. Require fresh task implementers and independent reviewers with raw RED/GREEN, separate fixes, and exact final validation.

## Review focus

- A thin slab can cut a face when every corner lies outside it; do not silently pass the blocked flux or sweep (Tasks 1, 2 and 4).
- A shared geometric face must produce one aperture and orientation for both adjacent rows. Changing the correction scale should break the physical identity (Task 2).
- A liquid/air center pair can put the interface within 0.05h of the liquid center. Require explicit regularization and correct signed capillary lift (Tasks 1 and 2).
- Wrong-side donors and missing pre-force history can make a plausible but incorrect affine fit. Check rank, same-side filtering, old/new and low-mass fallback separately (Task 3).
- A wall can cross a thin obstacle interval between endpoint samples while both velocities still point into the wall. Verify continuous earliest contact and separate corrections (Task 4).
- Device tests must execute the new numerical paths on the actual architecture and keep prior GPU/memcheck gates (Tasks 5 and 6).
