# Regular-Cell Liquid Transport Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development with fresh implementers and independent review. Steps use checkbox syntax. Keep RED, GREEN, and exact artifact identities in ignored evidence.

**Goal:** Preserve material volume in the existing incompressible stationary-tank workload by using a velocity reconstruction consistent with the projected face flux.

**Architecture:** Retain the quadratic APIC gather for stored velocity and affine history. In supported regular cells, reconstruct the transport velocity from the six projected face-normal velocities and use the derivative of that same field for the existing exponential volume update. Stage the six checked samples and local coordinates in the shared scheduler, then evaluate the same scalar arithmetic in the selected CPU or CUDA update. Use the established transport path outside the narrow eligibility contract.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, MSVC, nvcc targeting SM120, Conan 2, CMake Ninja presets, uv locked native validation.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-fluidity-design.md §§5.1, 6.1 and 8.3, docs/papers/fluidity-method.typ sections Affine particle–grid transfer and Pressure projection, and Tasks 5–7 of docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-09-real-3d-liquid-acceptance-and-throughput.md. This is a bounded transport refinement for regular stationary-tank cells. It replaces the original quadratic advection and positional separation term on eligible steps, while preserving the ASFLIP stored-velocity formula. Keep the original plan and its acceptance gates.

## Evidence and constraints

- The verified default CPU scene kept all 256 particles and grew material volume by 6.7561% through frame 48. The pressure rows met their local defect threshold. The independently reproduced six-face diagnostic held maximum drift to 0.0000841% through 240 frames and all 1,200 accepted steps. All three energy variants passed the unchanged frame-48 endpoint comparison. At frame 12 their endpoint front gap was 60.30 mm and kinetic energy gap 3.595 J. An earlier diagnostic process returned RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED at frame 20 and did not reproduce in the exact binary retry or subsequent longer replays.
- Only zero-target, zero-compliance, zero-surface-tension source-free transported sphere-union liquid in a stationary axis-aligned open tank may select this path. Every selected cell must belong to the frozen pressure problem. Partly filled liquid cells can qualify when all six solid apertures are fully open or exactly sealed at a certified stationary tank plane. A partially blocked face, unknown or unusable open sample, uncertifiable fraction, missing owner, moving wall, or non-tank scene does not qualify.
- Preserve the pressure solve, topology, source fields, contact operator, supported donor rules, current geometric field, and all committed wire formats. Keep the fitted APIC velocity and original old-field residual when computing stored velocity. Pass both stored and selected advection velocities once through existing swept contact. The new field has no positional separation residual. Update volume by J_new = J_old exp(dt div u_transport) using the same field as advection. Do not clamp or normalize J.
- This is the lowest-order face-flux-compatible interpolant on a Cartesian cell. Normal velocity matches across shared faces of two selected cells, and tangential velocity may be discontinuous. The discrete position step and measured material volume are separate from geometric Jacobian or receiving volume. Generic cut cells, moving piston, adaptive interfaces, free drops, viscosity, capillary and rendering are outside this change.
- Use current AGENTS.md and pinned toolchain. Host/device evaluation must preserve the X/Y/Z accumulation order and strict floating-point flags. Track actual scratch and arena bytes, including added per-particle records. Allocate nothing during an attempt. Keep exact GPU images and runtime checks separate.
